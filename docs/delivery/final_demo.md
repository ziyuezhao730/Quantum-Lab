final demo - four minutes

0:00-0:30 - the problem
students have several weak topics, overlapping study opportunities & approaching exams.
examace turns those competing choices into a schedule they can follow.

0:30-1:30 - use the app
complete the six-question dialog, set the budget & open availability/exams.
build a plan. show review before practice, breaks & sessions before deadlines.
change one exam day, rebuild & show the schedule change.
open Practice & reveal an answer. save the profile.

1:30-2:45 - the optimization
an individual session is a binary decision.
18 choices give 262144 possible combinations. overlaps & prerequisites couple the choices.
in one recorded case the exact schedule scored 12.815 & greedy scored 10.936.
we compare Qiskit QAOA with those classical references on the same exported problem.
Qiskit prepares the state, applies the mixer & samples schedules.
we improved the starting state after a short-budget case produced no feasible sample.

2:45-3:30 - measured results
show Saved example runs in Quantum lab.
27 runs cover three profiles, three problem sizes & three seeds.
all runs produced feasible schedules. average sampled score was 85.1% of exact.
mean feasible-shot rate increased from 2.8% to 8.2% after the starting-state change.
all results are retained, including weaker runs.

3:30-4:00 - close
working product - diagnostic, availability, deadlines, preparation, breaks & practice.
quantum contribution - binary model, Qiskit simulation & reproducible solver comparison.
this is a small fictional case study. further work would validate real course data,
scale the classical reference & evaluate deeper circuits.
