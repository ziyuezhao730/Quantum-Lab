# -ExamAce- (押题大师) | Qiskit Fall Fest 2026

Project repository for **Qiskit Fall Fest 2026**, an IBM Quantum hackathon!

## About the Project

ExamAce is an AI-powered exam preparation platform that combines intelligent document analysis with quantum optimization to help students prepare more effectively for exams.

Students can upload course materials, including past midterms, quizzes, assignments, lecture slides, and textbooks. The platform analyzes these materials to identify important topics, recurring assessment patterns, and relevant practice questions.

Using information about topic importance, individual proficiency, and available study time, ExamAce develops personalized study plans. We explore quantum optimization using Qiskit and the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) to determine how students can best allocate their limited study time.

## Objectives

- Develop an AI-powered system to analyze course materials and identify important exam topics.
- Extract, categorize, and retrieve relevant practice questions from uploaded educational resources.
- Generate personalized practice questions based on course content and student knowledge gaps.
- Formulate study-time allocation as a combinatorial optimization problem.
- Implement and experiment with QUBO formulations and QAOA using Qiskit.
- Compare quantum optimization results with classical optimization approaches.
